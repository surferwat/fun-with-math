Find the Galois group of $x^8 - 1= 0$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

Let $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/8}$.
Then the roots of $x^8 - 1 = 0$ are $1, \zeta, \zeta^2, \zeta^3, \zeta^4, \zeta^5, \zeta^6, \zeta^7$.
Since every root is a power of $\zeta$, the splitting field is $L = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta)$.
Now $\zeta^2 = i$, $\zeta + \zeta^{-1} = 2 \cos \frac{\pi}{4} = \sqrt{2}$, so $L = \mathbb{Q}(i, \sqrt{2})$.

Since $[\mathbb{Q}(i) : \mathbb{Q}]=2$ and $\sqrt{2} \notin \mathbb{Q}(i)$ we obtain $[L:\mathbb{Q}] = [\mathbb{Q}(i, \sqrt{2}): \mathbb{Q}(i)][\mathbb{Q}(i): \mathbb{Q}] = 2 \cdot 2 = 4$.
Hence the Galois group has order $4$.

Every automorphism $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q})$ fixes $\mathbb{Q}$, so $\sigma(\zeta)^8 = \sigma(\zeta^8) = 1$.
Thus $\sigma(\zeta)$ is an eighth root of unity. 
Since $\zeta$ generates $L$, its image must also generate $L$ so $\sigma(\zeta)$ must be a primitive eighth root of unity.
Therefore $\sigma(\zeta) = \zeta^k$, $k \in \left\{ 1,3,5,7 \right\}$.
Each choice of $k$ gives a distinct automorphism, and since there are four such choices, these are all the automorphisms of $L$. 
Their action on every root is determined by $\sigma(\zeta^m) = \sigma(\zeta)^m = (\zeta^k)^m = \zeta^{km}$, where the exponent is taken modulo $8$.

Thus the Galois group is presented by the substitutions
$1$ $\zeta$ $\zeta^2$ $\zeta^3$ $\zeta^4$ $\zeta^5$ $\zeta^6$ $\zeta^7$
$1$ $\zeta^3$ $\zeta^6$ $\zeta$ $\zeta^4$ $\zeta^7$ $\zeta^2$ $\zeta^5$
$1$ $\zeta^5$ $\zeta^2$ $\zeta^7$ $\zeta^4$ $\zeta$ $\zeta^6$ $\zeta^3$
$1$ $\zeta^7$ $\zeta^6$ $\zeta^5$ $\zeta^4$ $\zeta^3$ $\zeta^2$ $\zeta$

Notes
- Steps to finding the galois group are as follows: 1) find all the roots of the polynomial, 2) find the splitting field, 3) determine the degree of the splitting field, 4) determine how an automorphism can act on the roots, and 5) write each substitution of the roots.
