Give an alternate proof of the identity $r_{n}^{n} - \sigma_1r_n^{n-1}+ \sigma_2r_n^{n-2}- ... \pm \sigma_n = 0$ of $\S11$.

$\mathit{Proof}$
By Fundamental Theorem of Algebra, we get $G(x) = (x-r_1)(x-r_2)...(x-r_n)$.
Since $r_k$ is a root of this polynomial, we have $G(x)=0$.
Expanding the product gives $G(x)= x^n - \sigma_1 x^{n-1} + \sigma_2 x^{n-2} - \sigma_3 x^{n-3} + ... + (-1)^n\sigma_n$ where $\sigma_1=\sum r_i$, $\sigma_2 = \sum_{i<j} r_ir_j$, ..., $\sigma_n = r_1r_2...r_n$.
Let $x=r_n$.
$G(r_n)=0$ since the factor $r_n-r_n$ appears in $\prod_{i=1}^n (r_n-r_i)=0$
Substitute $x=r_n$ into the expanded form.
$G(r_n) = r_{n}^{n} - \sigma_1r_n^{n-1}+ \sigma_2r_n^{n-2}- ... \pm \sigma_n$.
Thus, $r_{n}^{n} - \sigma_1r_n^{n-1}+ \sigma_2r_n^{n-2}- ... \pm \sigma_n = 0$. $\blacksquare$
