In the case where the discriminant of the irreducible cubic $x^3 + px + q$ is not a square, give the explicit factorization of $F(X)$ (Exercise 8 of the Fourth Set) into linear factors over $K(t)$.

Since $f(x) = x^3 + px + q \in K[x]$ is irreducible, and its discriminant is not a square in $K$, it follows that the Galois group of $f$ is the full symmetric group $Gal(f) \cong S_3$, which has all six substitutions.
Now recall the definition of $F(X)$ in $\S 41$: $F(X)$ is the polynomial whose roots are the images of the Galois resolvent $t$ under all substitutions of the Galois group.
Since the Galois group has all six elements, $F(X)$ has exactly the six conjugates of $t$ as its roots. From Exercise 8, these are $t, -t, \omega t, -\omega^2 t, -\omega^2 t$.
Therefore, $F(X) = (X-t)(X+t)(X-\omega t)(X + \omega t)(X - \omega^2 t)(X + \omega^2t)$.
This is exactly the explicit factorization into linear factors.
