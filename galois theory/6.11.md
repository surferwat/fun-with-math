Deduce from Dedekind's theorem of Exercise 10 that if $f$ is a polynomial with coefficients in $K$ and if $K'$ is obtained from $K$ by adjoining all roots of a polynomial $g$ with coefficients in $K$ then all irreducible factors of $f$ over $K'$ have the same degree.

Let $K'$ be the splitting field of $g$ over $K$. 
Choose a Galois resolvent $u$ of $g$ such that $K'=K(u)$, and let $G(X)$ be the irreducible polynomial of $u$ over $K$. 
Let $a$ be a root of $f$ and define $K''=K(a)$. 
By Exercise 10, the irreducible factors of $G$ over $K''$ all have the same degree, since their degrees are determined by the substitutions in the Galois group of $g=0$ over $K''$. 
Now apply Dedekind's reciprocity theorem to the irreducible polynomials $f$ and $G$. 
Over $K'=K(u)$, write $f=f_1f_2\cdots f_\mu$, and over $K''=K(a)$, write $G=G_1G_2\cdots G_\mu$, with the factors paired according to Dedekind's theorem. 
Then $\frac{\deg f_k}{\deg f}=\frac{\deg G_k}{\deg G}$ for every $k$. 
Since all the $G_k$ have the same degree, $\frac{\deg G_k}{\deg G}$ is independent of $k$. 
Therefore $\deg f_k$ is independent of $k$. 
Hence all irreducible factors of $f$ over $K'$ have the same degree.

Author's solution
If $K'$ can be obtained from $K$ by adjoining a Galois resolvent $u$ of $g$ over $K$. Let $G(X) = 0$ be the irreducible polynomial with coefficients in $K$ of which $u$ is a root. If $K''$ is an extension of $K$ then the irreducible factors of $G$ over $K''$ all have the same degree, namely, the number of substitutions in the Galois group of $g=0$ over $K''$. Apply Dedekind's theorem in the case $K'=K(u)$, $K''=K(a)$ where $a$ root of $f$. 

Notes
- In regards to $g$, we are specifically told to take $K'$ to be the field obtained by adjoining all roots of $g$. Thus $g$ determines the extension $K'/K$. In regards to $f$, we are not told to adjoin its roots. Instead, we want to determine how $f$ factors over the already-chosen field $K'$. In other words, $g$ is used to construct the extension, $G$ is introduced as a convenient polynomial associated with that extension, $f$ is the polynomial whose factorization we ultimately want to understand.
- Conceptually, Exercise 10 gives us "equal degrees for the factors of $G$," and Dedekind's Reciprocity Theorem transfers that equality to the factors of $f$.
- "$G(X)$ is the irreducible polynomial of $u$ over $K$," we mean that $G(X)$ is a polynomial in the variable $X$ whose coefficients are in $K$, and $u$ is a root of $G$: $G(u) = 0$.
- We know that $\frac{\deg G_k}{\deg G}$ is independent of $k$. Therefore $\frac{\deg f_k}{\deg f}$ must also be independent of $k$. Now $\deg f$ is a fixed number: $f$ is one fixed polynomial. So if $\frac{\deg f_k}{\deg f}=c$ for the same constant $c$ for every $k$, then multiplying both sides by $\deg f$ gives $\deg f_k=c\deg f$. Since both $c$ and $\deg f$ are independent of $k$, $\deg f_k$ is also independent of $k$.
